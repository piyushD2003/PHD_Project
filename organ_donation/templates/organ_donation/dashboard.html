About Organ Donation

Organ donation is a generous gift that can save lives. Through this system, you can:

  • Register organs you wish to donate
  • Request organs when needed
  • Match donors with recipients safely
  • Track donation status in real-time
  • Work with qualified medical professionals
Donation Process
  1. Registration: Register your organ donation
  2. Verification: Medical verification of organ health
  3. Matching: System finds compatible recipients
  4. Direct Contact: Donor and recipient communicate directly via phone
  5. Transaction: Organ transplant procedure
  6. Follow-up: Post-transplant care and monitoring
